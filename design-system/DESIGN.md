### Posture rules
- Accent budget: at most 2 visible uses of --accent #E89B3C per screen; never on hover/focus rings of a --brand element
- Brand navy #0E2A47 drives primary actions; viking amber #E89B3C is the polaris signal
- Component kit covers: btn_primary, btn_secondary, card, card_subtle, input, modal, page, chip_brand, chip_accent, chip_default, divider, divider_strong
- Controls are 44px minimum touch targets with hover (L ±0.06–0.12), active and :focus-visible states; disabled is the only state allowed to reduce contrast
- Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (body) and ≥ 3:1 (large text / icons) in every state; body copy max-width 65ch
- Dark mode mirrors tokens.css [data-theme=dark]: bg #0A1A2F, surface #102742, brand #6FA0E0, amber #F2B260
- Radii: 6px chips / 8px default / 12px panels / 16px quote / 20px cards / pill badges; container 1200px
- Background: strictly cool ice/fjord family — never warm beige/peach/pink
